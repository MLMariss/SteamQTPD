Player counts

How many people are in a game on Steam right now, recorded about once an hour, and which games are spiking against their own last week.

Trending

The last 24 hours' peak is at least 1.5× the game's usual daily peak over the earlier days, and at least 200 players. Biggest jump first.

Newly tracked

Games that just joined the watchlist with 200+ players and no earlier day to compare with yet. Biggest peak first.

Most played

Steam's top 100 by players now. Ranks are Steam's own; a gap is an app the QTPD catalogue does not hold, such as software.

Data: players.json, written by players.py (workflow 2.14) from Steam's public player counts. Every game in the catalogue is checked every 2 hours, and any with 50+ players is then followed hourly for a week, along with Steam's top 100 and games released in the last 90 days with 100+ reviews. Adult games are not tracked.
